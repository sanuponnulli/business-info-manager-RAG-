2. Our values and code of conduct

Our values

  • Customers first, always honestly. We tell customers what Acme Flow can and cannot do.
  • Write it down. Decisions, plans and handovers live in writing so colleagues in other time zones can follow.
  • Own the outcome. If you see a problem, you own it until it reaches the right person.
  • Kind and direct. Give clear feedback, with respect. Assume good intent.
  • Leave it better. Improve the code, the process or the document you touched.

Code of conduct

Everyone at Acme is expected to act with integrity, follow the law in every country where we operate, and treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. In particular:

  • Conflicts of interest. Tell your manager and HR in writing about any outside business, investment or relationship that could influence your decisions at Acme, including a close relationship with a colleague you manage or a supplier you choose.
  • Outside work. You may take on paid work outside Acme only if it does not compete with Acme, does not use Acme time or equipment, and your manager has approved it in writing. Contract staff should check their own contract.
  • Gifts and hospitality. You may accept gifts or hospitality worth up to USD 100 (or the local equivalent) per person per year from any one company. Anything above that must be declared to Finance at finance@acme.test, and cash or vouchers must always be declined.
  • Anti-bribery. Never offer, pay or accept a bribe or a "facilitation payment", whatever the amount. Report any request to legal@acme.test immediately.
  • Confidentiality. Customer data, unreleased product plans, salaries and financial results are confidential. Do not share them outside Acme, and inside Acme only with people who need them for their work.
  • Social media. You are welcome to say you work at Acme. Do not speak on Acme's behalf, share confidential information, or post about customers without their permission. Press questions go to marketing@acme.test.

Breaking the code of conduct may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal.

3. Working hours, attendance and remote work

Working hours

A full-time working week is 40 hours, Monday to Friday, including a one-hour lunch break each day. Part-time staff work the hours in their contract, which is normally 60% of full time (24 hours a week).

Each office has core hours when everyone is expected to be reachable for meetings:

Outside core hours you can arrange your day flexibly with your team. Please keep your calendar up to date so colleagues in other time zones can see when you are available.

Overtime

We do not expect regular overtime. If a release or customer incident needs you to work late or at a weekend, agree it with your manager in advance where possible. Weekend or public holiday work is compensated with time off in lieu: one day off for each day worked, to be taken within 60 days. Where local law requires overtime pay (for example for some roles in the UAE and India), Payroll pays it instead.

Attendance and lateness

If you will be late or cannot work on a given day, tell your manager before your normal start time, by message or phone. If you are ill, follow the sick leave rules in section 4.

Remote and hybrid work

Acme is a hybrid company. The default is three office days a week (Tuesday, Wednesday and Thursday are the team days) and up to two days working from home.

  • Fully remote roles exist only where the job offer says so.
  • Working from home requires a quiet place to work, a reliable internet connection of at least 20 Mbps, and that you can join video calls with your camera on.
  • Home office allowance. Everyone who works from home at least one day a week can claim a one-off home office allowance of AED 1,500, INR 25,000 or GBP 300 for a chair, desk or monitor, through the expenses process.
  • Working from another country. You may work from outside the country of your contract for up to 20 working days per calendar year, with your manager's approval and HR's confirmation at least two weeks before you travel. Longer periods create tax and immigration issues and are not allowed.
  • Remote work is a privilege, not a right. A manager can ask a team member to come to the office more often for onboarding, a project, or performance reasons, with one week's notice.

New joiners work from the office every working day for their first four weeks, to meet the team and settle in.

4. Leave: annual, sick, personal, parental and unpaid

Acme has five types of leave. Annual, sick and personal leave have a yearly balance. Parental and unpaid leave do not; they are approved case by case. The leave year is the calendar year, 1 January to 31 December.

4.1 Leave entitlements by country

Entitlements depend on the country of your office and are counted in working days. Weekends and public holidays inside a leave period do not count against your balance.

Part-time staff

Part-time staff receive 60% of the full-time annual, sick and personal leave. Sick and personal leave are rounded to the nearest whole day. For example, a part-time employee in Kochi gets 14.4 days of annual leave, 7 days of sick leave and 2 days of personal leave.

Contract staff

Fixed-term contract staff receive 12 days of annual leave per year in every country, plus the full sick and personal leave for their country, unless their contract says otherwise.

Joining during the year

If you join during the year, your annual leave for that first year is pro-rated by the number of whole months left in the year, counting the month you join, and rounded to the nearest half day. For example, someone in Dubai who joins in July gets 30 × 6 ÷ 12 = 15 days of annual leave for that year. Sick and personal leave are not pro-rated.

4.2 Annual leave

Annual leave is for holidays, rest and anything else you choose. We want everyone to take their full annual leave each year; managers will remind you if you have taken little leave by September.

  • Annual leave is paid at your normal base pay plus allowances.
  • The smallest unit of leave is half a day.
  • You may not take more than 15 consecutive working days of annual leave at once without your department head's approval.
  • Annual leave cannot be exchanged for money, except for untaken leave when you leave Acme (see section 17).

4.3 Carry-over

You may carry over up to 5 days of unused annual leave into the next year. Carried-over days are added to the next year's balance on 1 January and can be used at any time during that year. Any unused annual leave above 5 days is lost on 31 December, so plan ahead. Sick and personal leave do not carry over.

How your balance is calculated. Remaining days = days entitled this year + days carried over − days already taken. Days in requests that are still pending are not yet subtracted, so your available days are the remaining days minus your pending requests. The Acme Assistant and the HR app show both numbers.

4.4 Sick leave

If you are too unwell to work, tell your manager before your normal start time on the first day, and record the absence as sick leave in the HR app on the day you return at the latest.

  • Medical certificate. For absences of up to three consecutive working days you do not need a doctor's note. If you are off sick for more than three consecutive working days, you must provide a medical certificate from a licensed doctor, uploaded to the HR app within two working days of returning.
  • Paid sick leave is paid at full base pay plus allowances, up to your yearly sick leave balance (15, 12 or 10 days by country).
  • Longer illness. If you need more sick leave than your balance, talk to HR. Further time off is covered first by any statutory sick pay in your country (for example UK Statutory Sick Pay), then by annual leave if you choose, and then by unpaid leave. HR will agree a plan with you and your manager, and may ask for an occupational health assessment.
  • Medical appointments that take less than half a day do not need to be booked as leave; just tell your manager. Longer appointments are booked as half a day of sick leave.
  • Caring for a sick child or partner is covered by personal leave, not sick leave.
  • Falling ill during annual leave: if you have a medical certificate, the sick days are converted to sick leave and your annual leave days are given back.

4.5 Personal leave

Personal leave is 3 days a year for things that cannot be done outside working hours: moving house, a visa or embassy appointment, paperwork at the bank, a child's school event, caring for a sick family member, or a family emergency. It can be taken at short notice, in half days, and does not need a reason beyond a short note.

Compassionate leave

If a close family member dies (partner, child, parent, sibling or grandparent), you can take up to 5 working days of paid compassionate leave, in addition to your personal leave. Tell your manager; HR records the days and they do not reduce any of your balances. More time can be agreed with HR.

4.6 Parental leave

Acme's parental leave policy is the same in every country, and applies to birth, adoption and surrogacy, for parents of any gender. It has no yearly balance; it is approved once per child.

Up to 10 working days a year needs your manager's approval; longer periods (up to three months) also need your department head and HR. During unpaid leave you are not paid, and benefits continue, but the company does not make pension contributions for the unpaid period.

5. Requesting and approving leave

How to request leave

  1. Check your balance in the HR app, or ask the Acme Assistant ("How many days of annual leave do I have left?").
  2. Agree the dates informally with your team, especially for leave of a week or longer.
  3. Submit the request in the HR app with the leave type, dates and a short reason. The request shows as pending.
  4. Your direct manager approves or rejects it. You get a notification either way. If your manager is away, the request goes to their manager.

Notice periods

LeaveMinimum notice
Annual leave of 1 to 3 days3 working days
Annual leave of more than 3 days2 weeks
Annual leave of more than 10 days4 weeks
Sick leave and personal leaveAs soon as you can, by your normal start time
Parental leave8 weeks (see section 4.6)

For managers: approving leave

  • Respond to leave requests within 3 working days. Requests not answered within 5 working days are escalated to your manager.
  • Approve requests unless there is a real business reason not to. Valid reasons include a release week, team coverage too low because others are already away, or the quarterly planning offsite. Always give the reason in the decision note and suggest other dates.
  • Managers can see the leave requests and balances of their direct reports only. HR can see everyone's. Use the HR app or ask the Acme Assistant ("Who in my team is off next week?" or "Which requests are waiting for my approval?").
  • Sick leave does not need approval; it is recorded, not requested.

Blackout periods

Each department may set up to two blackout weeks per quarter, for example around major releases or the quarterly planning offsite. Blackout weeks are announced at least six weeks in advance. Annual leave in a blackout week needs your department head's approval.

Cancelling or changing leave

You can cancel a pending or approved request in the HR app at any time before it starts; the days go back to your balance. To change the dates of approved leave, cancel it and submit a new request. If leave has already started, ask your manager to end it early; HR adjusts the record. Acme will only ask you to cancel approved leave in an emergency, and will reimburse any non-refundable travel or booking costs if it does.

7. Pay and payroll

Payday

Everyone is paid monthly, on the 25th, for the whole calendar month, in the currency of their office (AED, INR or GBP) by bank transfer. Your payslip is available in the HR app on payday. If you join or leave part-way through a month, you are paid for the calendar days you were employed in that month.

Changes that affect pay (new bank details, unpaid leave, expense claims, address changes for tax) must reach Payroll by the 15th of the month to be included that month; anything later is processed the following month.

Allowances by country

  • UAE: a housing and transport allowance of 30% of base pay, paid monthly.
  • India: a house rent allowance (HRA) and flexible benefits allowance totalling 25% of base pay, paid monthly. You can declare rent receipts to Payroll to reduce the tax withheld.
  • UK: no percentage allowance. London-based staff receive a London weighting of GBP 250 a month.

Tax and deductions by country

  • UAE: there is no personal income tax and no employee deductions. Gross pay equals net pay.
  • India: income tax (TDS) is withheld every month under the tax regime you choose in April. Deductions are your Employees' Provident Fund contribution of 12% of base pay (Acme adds a matching contribution) and professional tax of INR 200 a month.
  • UK: income tax and National Insurance are withheld through PAYE. Deductions are your workplace pension contribution of 5% of base pay (Acme adds 5%; see section 8).

Annual bonus

Permanent staff are eligible for an annual performance bonus, paid with the March payroll for the previous calendar year. The bonus is normally between 4% and 15% of annual base salary, depending on Acme's results and your performance review rating. To be eligible you must have joined before 1 January of the year being assessed and still be employed, and not under notice, on 1 March. Contract staff are not eligible for the annual bonus.

Salary reviews

Salaries are reviewed once a year. New salaries take effect from the April payroll, based on the annual performance review, market data and Acme's results. A promotion during the year can come with a salary change from the month it takes effect.

Problems with your pay

If your payslip looks wrong, write to payroll@acme.test within 30 days with your employee code and the month. Underpayments are corrected within 5 working days by a separate transfer. Overpayments are recovered from the next payroll, or in instalments if the amount is large; Payroll will agree this with you first.

Salary advances

In an emergency you can ask for a one-time salary advance of up to one month's net pay, repaid over a maximum of six months. Requests go to your manager and then Finance.

9. Expenses and business travel

General rules

  • Spend Acme's money as if it were your own. Choose the reasonable option, not the most expensive one.
  • Get approval from your manager before spending more than USD 200 (or local equivalent) on anything.
  • Submit expense claims in the expenses app with a receipt for every item within 30 days of the expense. Claims older than 90 days are not reimbursed.
  • Approved claims are reimbursed with the next payroll if submitted by the 15th of the month.
  • Alcohol, fines (parking or speeding), personal entertainment and upgrades are not reimbursed.

Business travel

  • All business travel must be approved by your manager and booked through Acme's travel desk (travel@acme.test) at least 14 days in advance where possible.
  • Flights: economy class for flights under 6 hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more. Business class needs approval from an executive.
  • Hotels: up to USD 180 a night in most cities, USD 250 in London and Dubai. Book through the travel desk.
  • Per diem: for meals and small expenses while travelling, you receive a daily allowance instead of claiming receipts: AED 250 in the UAE, INR 3,000 in India, GBP 60 in the UK, and USD 75 elsewhere. Meals paid by Acme or a customer reduce the per diem by a third each.
  • Local transport: use public transport or a standard taxi or ride-hailing service. Mileage for your own car is paid at AED 1.0, INR 12 or GBP 0.45 per kilometre/mile as appropriate.
  • Travel insurance is provided automatically for business trips. Tell the travel desk about any visa needs at least four weeks before you travel.

Team events and client entertainment

Each team has a social budget of USD 40 per person per quarter. Client entertainment must be approved by your manager in advance and is limited to USD 75 per person, including Acme staff attending.

10. Equipment, IT and information security

Your equipment

On your first day you receive a laptop (MacBook Pro for Engineering and Design, MacBook Air or Windows laptop for other teams), a headset, and access to the tools you need. Engineers may also request a test phone. Equipment remains Acme property and must be returned when you leave.

Laptops are replaced every four years, or sooner if they fail. Request new equipment or repairs through the IT helpdesk at it@acme.test.

Security rules everyone must follow

  • Use the company password manager and a unique password of at least 14 characters for every account.
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required on all company accounts. Use the authenticator app, not SMS.
  • Lock your screen whenever you leave your laptop, even in the office. Laptops lock automatically after 5 minutes.
  • Keep your operating system and apps up to date; IT pushes updates and you must restart within 3 days of being asked.
  • Never install pirated software or browser extensions that IT has not approved.
  • Customer data must stay in approved systems. Never copy production data to your laptop, a personal account or a USB drive.
  • Do not use public Wi-Fi without the company VPN.

Phishing and incidents

If you receive a suspicious email or message, do not click links or open attachments. Use the "Report phishing" button in your email, or forward it to security@acme.test. If you clicked something, tell the security team straight away: reporting quickly matters far more than the mistake, and no one is punished for reporting honestly.

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to security@acme.test within 24 hours, so the device can be wiped remotely.

Using AI tools

You may use approved AI tools, including the Acme Assistant, for your work. Do not paste customer data, source code that is not open source, or other confidential information into AI tools that IT has not approved. You remain responsible for checking anything an AI tool produces before you use it.

Personal use

Reasonable personal use of your laptop and the internet is fine, as long as it is legal, does not interfere with your work, and does not involve storing large personal files. Acme may monitor company systems for security purposes, in line with section 16.

12. Performance, probation and promotions

Probation

New permanent employees have a probation period of six months (three months for contract staff). Your manager meets you monthly during probation and confirms the outcome in writing before the end. Probation can be extended once, by up to three months. During probation the notice period is shorter (section 17).

Performance reviews

Acme runs two review cycles a year:

  • Mid-year check-in (July): a conversation with your manager about progress, goals and development. No rating.
  • Annual review (January): self-review, peer feedback from two to four colleagues, and your manager's review, ending with a rating that informs your bonus (paid in March) and salary review (from April).

Ratings are: Exceptional, Strong, Solid, Developing and Not yet meeting expectations. Ratings are calibrated across each department so they are fair between teams.

One-to-ones

Every employee has a one-to-one with their manager at least every two weeks. It is your meeting: bring topics about your work, your growth, or anything that is in your way.

Promotions

Promotions are decided twice a year, in January and July, based on the career framework for your role. Your manager proposes the promotion with written evidence; a panel from your department reviews it. You can ask your manager at any time what you need to show to reach the next level.

Performance improvement

If performance falls below expectations, your manager will first discuss it with you informally. If it does not improve, a written performance improvement plan of 6 to 8 weeks sets clear goals and support. HR is involved at this stage.

13. Dress code and workplace

Dress code

Acme's dress code is smart casual in all offices. Jeans, chinos, shirts, t-shirts without offensive slogans, and clean trainers are all fine. Dress for comfort and for the day you have ahead.

  • Religious and cultural dress is always welcome, including head coverings.
  • Fridays are fully casual in every office, unless you have a customer meeting.
  • Safety footwear is required in the Dubai warehouse area and server room.

Our offices

  • Desks are shared (hot desking). Book a desk in the office app on your team days and clear it at the end of the day.
  • Quiet zones are for focused work; take calls in phone booths or meeting rooms.
  • Food is available in every office: free breakfast on team days, fruit and drinks all day.
  • Visitors must be registered at reception and accompanied at all times.
  • Smoking and vaping are only allowed in the designated outdoor areas.

14. Health, safety and wellbeing

Health and safety

Everyone is responsible for keeping our workplaces safe. Report any hazard, accident or near miss to the office manager on the same day. Each office has trained first aiders and fire wardens, listed at reception and in the office app. Fire drills take place twice a year.

If you work from home, complete the home workstation self-assessment in your first month. IT and HR can help you with equipment such as an external monitor, keyboard or chair (see the home office allowance in section 3).

Wellbeing

  • Employee Assistance Programme: free, confidential counselling (section 8), available 24 hours a day.
  • Mental health first aiders in every office: trained colleagues you can talk to in confidence.
  • No-meeting Fridays after 14:00 local time, to protect focus time.
  • Right to disconnect: you are not expected to read or answer messages outside your working hours, except when you are on call. Schedule messages for the recipient's working hours where you can.

On-call

Engineers in the on-call rota receive an on-call allowance of AED 300, INR 4,000 or GBP 75 per week on call, plus time off in lieu for any incident work outside working hours longer than two hours.

15. Respect at work, grievances and whistleblowing

Equal opportunity and respect

Acme does not tolerate discrimination, harassment or bullying of any kind, including on the basis of gender, race, nationality, religion, age, disability, sexual orientation, marital or parental status. This applies in the office, at events, on business trips and online.

Harassment includes unwanted conduct that violates someone's dignity or creates an intimidating, hostile or offensive environment, including jokes, comments, messages and physical contact.

Raising a concern or grievance

  1. Informally: where you feel comfortable, raise the issue directly with the person or with your manager.
  2. Formally: write to your HR Business Partner or to hr@acme.test. HR acknowledges your grievance within 2 working days and arranges a meeting within 7 working days. You may bring a colleague to the meeting.
  3. Appeal: if you disagree with the outcome, you can appeal in writing within 10 working days to the Head of People, who appoints someone not previously involved.

Whistleblowing

If you suspect fraud, bribery, a serious safety risk, a data breach that is being hidden, or any illegal activity, report it to ethics@acme.test, or anonymously through the independent ethics hotline listed in the office app. Reports go directly to the Chief Financial Officer and an independent board member. Acme protects anyone who raises a concern in good faith from any retaliation.

16. Your personal data and who can see it

Acme holds personal data about you to employ you and pay you: your contact details, contract, pay, bank details, leave, benefits and performance reviews. We handle it in line with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and the UK GDPR.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the personal data Acme holds about you, ask for mistakes to be corrected, and, in some cases, ask for data to be deleted. Write to privacy@acme.test; we respond within 30 days. Employee records are kept for 6 years after you leave, then deleted, unless the law requires longer.

Untaken annual leave

Annual leave you have built up but not taken is paid in your final salary, calculated pro rata for the part of the year you worked. If you have taken more leave than you have earned, the extra days are deducted from your final pay. Your manager may ask you to use remaining leave during your notice period.

End-of-service gratuity (UAE)

Employees in the UAE with at least one year of continuous service receive an end-of-service gratuity as required by UAE labour law: 21 days of basic salary for each of the first five years, and 30 days of basic salary for each further year, up to a maximum of two years' total salary. It is paid with your final salary.

Gratuity (India)

Employees in India with at least five years of continuous service receive gratuity under the Payment of Gratuity Act: 15 days of the last drawn basic salary for each completed year of service.

Your last days

  • Hand over your work in writing, in the team's documentation space.
  • Return your laptop, phone, access card and any other equipment on your last day.
  • HR invites you to an exit interview; your honest feedback helps us improve.
  • You receive a final payslip, a service letter confirming your dates and role, and, in the UK, a P45.
  • Health cover ends on your last day of employment (at the end of that month in the UK).

18. Using the Acme Assistant

The Acme Assistant is our AI helper, available in the Acme mobile app by text or voice. It answers questions from this handbook and from your own HR records.

What you can ask

  • Policies: "What is the dress code?", "Do I need a doctor's note for two sick days?", "How does carry-over work?"
  • Your own records: "How many days of annual leave do I have left?", "When is my next public holiday?", "What was my net pay last month?", "Which benefits am I enrolled in?"
  • The directory: "Who is the finance manager?", "Find Arjun in Engineering."
  • Managers: "Which leave requests are waiting for my approval?", "Who in my team is away next week?"

Good to know

  • Policy answers show the handbook page they come from, so you can check the source.
  • The assistant can read your records but cannot yet submit, change or approve anything. Use the HR app for that.
  • It only sees what you are allowed to see (section 16). If it tells you it cannot access something, that is the access rule working, not a fault.
  • If an answer looks wrong, check the source and tell HR. The handbook and your HR records are the official reference, not the assistant.

19. Frequently asked questions

How many sick days do I get?

15 paid sick days a year in the UAE, 12 in India and 10 in the UK (60% of that for part-time staff). You need a medical certificate if you are off sick for more than three consecutive working days. See section 4.4.

Can I carry over unused annual leave?

Yes, up to 5 days into the next year. Anything above 5 days is lost on 31 December. See section 4.3.

Why does my available leave differ from my remaining leave?

Available leave also subtracts requests that are still pending approval. Once a request is approved it counts as used; if it is rejected or cancelled the days come back.

Can I work from home every day?

Not by default. Acme is hybrid: three office days (Tuesday to Thursday) and up to two days at home, unless your offer says the role is fully remote. You can work from another country for up to 20 working days a year with approval. See section 3.

When is payday?

The 25th of every month. Payslips appear in the HR app the same day. See section 7.

When is the bonus paid?

With the March payroll, for the previous year. It is normally 4% to 15% of base salary, for permanent staff only. See section 7.

Do I pay for health insurance?

Not for yourself: Employee-only health cover is free in every country. Adding a partner or family has a monthly cost shown in section 8.

Is there a pension?

In the UK, the workplace pension (you 5%, Acme 5%). In India, the Provident Fund (12% each) plus an optional NPS with up to 10% from Acme. In the UAE, an end-of-service gratuity instead. See sections 8 and 17.

What is the dress code?

Smart casual, fully casual on Fridays, and business attire for customer meetings in Dubai. See section 13.

What do I do if I lose my laptop?

Report it to security@acme.test within 24 hours so it can be wiped. See section 10.

Who approves my leave if my manager is on holiday?

Your manager's manager. Requests not answered within 5 working days are escalated automatically. See section 5.

How much is the per diem when I travel?

AED 250 a day in the UAE, INR 3,000 in India, GBP 60 in the UK, and USD 75 elsewhere. See section 9.

Can I see my teammates' leave?

Only if you are their manager (direct reports only) or in HR. Colleagues see directory information only. See section 16.
